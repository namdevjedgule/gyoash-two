Refund & Cancellation Policy

Our refund and cancellation terms explain return eligibility, refund processing, order cancellations, and what to do if you need assistance with an order.

GYOASH TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.

01

Refund Policy

01

Eligibility for Refund

  • Products must be returned in unused, resalable condition within 7 days of delivery.
  • Electronic/Software Products are non-refundable once activated.
02

How to Request a Refund

  • Email service@gyoash.com with your order number, date of purchase, and reason for return.
  • We will provide a Return Authorization (RA) number and shipping instructions.
service@gyoash.com
03

Processing Refunds

  • Once we receive and inspect the returned Product, we will notify you of approval or rejection.
  • Approved refunds will be processed within 10 business days via the original payment method.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable.
04

Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be granted for Products not in original condition, damaged, or missing parts.

05

Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven’t received a refund within 15 days, check with your bank or credit card company, then contact us at service@gyoash.com .

02

Cancellation Policy

01

Order Cancellation by You

  • You may cancel an order before it has been dispatched by emailing us at service@gyoash.com .
  • If the order has already been dispatched, our Refund Policy applies.
02

Order Cancellation by Us

  • We may cancel orders due to product unavailability, pricing errors, or suspicious activity.
  • If we cancel your order after payment, you will receive a full refund within 10 business days.
03

How to Cancel

  • Email service@gyoash.com with your order details and cancellation request.
  • We will confirm cancellation via email.
04

Contact Us
