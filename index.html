About Gyoash

Smart technology solutions for a digital-first future

Gyoash is India's go-to partner for interactive panels, classroom management software and enterprise AV — headquartered in Pune, with teams on the ground in Nashik, Indore and Nagpur.

We believe technology should be accessible to every institution. Our work spans digital classrooms, interactive panels, LED walls and enterprise IT - engineered, installed and supported by one accountable team.
