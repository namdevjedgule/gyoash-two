Shipping Policy

How we dispatch, deliver, and install Gyoash products — and what to do if something arrives damaged or missing.

GYOASH TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.

01

Delivery Coverage

We currently deliver across India. For international shipping inquiries, please contact our team at sales.india@gyoash.com or +91 91586 20055.

02

Shipping Timeline

Dispatch

All orders are processed and dispatched within 4–5 business days from the date of order confirmation.

Delivery

Estimated delivery time is 15–20 business days, depending on the delivery location. Remote or rural locations may require additional time.

03

Shipping Charges

A nominal standard shipping fee will be added at checkout, as applicable based on location and weight.

04

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email or SMS with a tracking number. You can track the status of your shipment using our logistics partner's tracking page.

05

Delivery Partners

We partner with reputed logistics service providers to ensure safe and timely delivery.

06

Delivery Attempts

Our delivery partners will make up to two delivery attempts. If the recipient is unavailable, the package will be returned to our warehouse. Re-delivery can be arranged upon request with applicable charges.

07

Damaged or Missing Items

If your order is delivered in a damaged condition or items are missing:

  • Please raise a complaint within 48 hours of delivery
  • Contact us at sales.india@gyoash.com or +91 91586 20055
  • Include your order number, photos of the package/product, and a brief description
08

Installation & Demonstration

For eligible products (like interactive flat panels), free on-site installation and demonstration is provided. Our support team will coordinate with you post-delivery for scheduling.

09

Contact Us
