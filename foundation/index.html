About The Foundation

Program design and implementation support, done properly

Gyoash Innovation Foundation (GIF) is a not-for-profit organisation incorporated as a Section 8 Company under the Companies Act, 2013. The Foundation is established to design and support impact-driven social development initiatives, with a primary focus on education, digital inclusion, and community capacity building across India.

GIF functions as a program design and implementation support organisation, working collaboratively with registered NGOs, institutions, and community-based entities to address priority development needs in a structured, measurable, and compliant manner.

CSR Partnerships

Collaborating for compliant & high-impact social initiatives

Gyoash Innovation Foundation collaborates with corporates, CSR-eligible NGOs, and institutions to support the design, structuring, and monitoring of CSR initiatives aligned with national priorities.

Important CSR Clarification

  • GIF does not directly receive CSR funds
  • GIF is not a CSR-eligible implementing agency
  • All CSR financial flows remain directly between corporates and CSR-eligible implementing entities

All CSR financial transactions remain outside GIF. Our role in every CSR initiative follows the same structure outlined in our Operating Model above — program design, implementation support, and monitoring & documentation. Never as a fund recipient.

Tax Benefits on Donations

Gyoash Innovation Foundation has received provisional registration under Section 12A, and provisional approval under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961. Eligible donations qualify for tax deduction under Section 80G, subject to applicable provisions of law.

Transparency & Accountability

Ethical governance, statutory compliance, audit readiness

GIF adheres to ethical governance, statutory compliance, transparent fund utilization, proper documentation, and audit readiness at every stage.

Donations are voluntary contributions for charitable purposes. Tax benefits are available only as per Section 80G provisions. Donors are advised to retain donation receipts for income-tax purposes.
